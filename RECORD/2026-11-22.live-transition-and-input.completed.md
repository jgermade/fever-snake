# Live world transition and quick turns

Follow-up to `2026-11-22.continuous-transition.completed.md` (PR #2 feedback).

## 2026-11-22

- Feedback: the transition did not feel continuous; quick turns such as
  down+right while heading left were lost.
- Transition: the old world is no longer a frozen snapshot. Both world
  instances stay alive for 1.6 s. The old world keeps rendering outside a
  growing portal (canvas clip) while its snake keeps crawling along its own
  path into the portal, one segment per tick; the new world renders inside,
  where the same snake unrolls. 3D worlds are never adjacent, so at most one
  world uses the shared WebGL canvas: a 3D old world renders below with the
  new 2D world clipped inside the portal on top; otherwise the old 2D world is
  drawn on top clipped to the outside of the portal.
- Placement: 2D worlds expose `screenOf(cell)`; after an invisible layout pass
  the snake is placed at the cell closest to the old head's screen position
  (falls back to normalised board position). The tick rhythm is not reset.
- Input: a reverse turn in the queue is kept for the next tick when a valid
  turn follows it, so "right" + "down" pressed together while heading left
  becomes down then right. A lone reverse press is still dropped. Tests added.
- Verified: 17 unit tests pass; headless Chromium tour through all 8 worlds
  and back to world 1 without errors; mid-transition screenshots reviewed.
