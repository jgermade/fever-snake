# "Go wild": FEVER mode, soundtrack, juice, three stranger worlds

## 2026-11-22

- Request: "Go wild" (creative freedom).
- FEVER mode (the game's namesake): apples less than 3.5 s apart build a
  combo; 3 in a row starts FEVER for 7 s (x3 points, +1.5 s per apple up to
  12 s, ~15% faster, rainbow hue-rotate filter over both canvases, meter in the
  HUD, music up an octave and 30% faster). Golden apples (12%) give +5 and
  instant FEVER; a star in the HUD and sparkles (2D worlds) mark them.
- Soundtrack: `src/music.js` composes a deterministic loop per world from its
  id (scale, root, bpm, bass/lead/kick/hat patterns) and schedules it with a
  WebAudio look-ahead sequencer. Stops on pause/game over; respects mute.
- Juice: `src/juice.js` particle bursts, floating "+N xC" popups, screen
  shake (CSS transform on the canvases), drawn over any world.
- Core hooks: `Snake.step` accepts a Set of apple keys and a `collides`
  rule; worlds may define `appleCells`, `collides`, `tickScale`.
- New worlds (21 total): Klein Bottle (`MobiusTopology` with `wrapX`, ghost
  of the head at glued edges), Black Hole (lensing warp r' = sqrt(r² + k²) with
  swirl, deadly horizon, gravitational time dilation via `tickScale`), Mirror
  Twins (two mirrored snakes; symmetric topology walls, ghost obstacles on the
  far side; either twin eats; twins colliding kills).
- Tests: 37 pass (apple sets, collision hook, Klein wrap, per-world music is
  deterministic and distinct). Chromium: new worlds survive their spawn,
  FEVER triggers on the 3rd consecutive apple, golden sparkle renders, no
  console errors.
