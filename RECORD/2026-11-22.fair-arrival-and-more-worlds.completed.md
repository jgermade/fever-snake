# Fair arrivals and five more worlds

## 2026-11-22

- Requests: more worlds; the world change must leave room — never land
  right in front of a wall or obstacle.
- Fair arrival (`src/placement.js`, unit-tested): candidates near the old
  on-screen position are scored by free runway ahead (up to 10 cells, bonus
  at >= 7), safe perpendicular escapes, keeping the heading, and a small
  distance cost. The first 6 ticks after a world change run slower (~1.9x
  tick time easing back). Worlds with random walls fall back to the same
  placement if their authored spawn is too tight.
  Verified in Chromium: hands off from the eating tick for 1.4 s across 13
  distinct world changes, no crash on arrival; all 18 worlds survive their own
  spawn with no input.
- Bug found while testing: the first rAF timestamp can precede the script
  start, giving a negative `dt`; Terminal's boot text then indexed line -1
  and threw, which stopped the game loop for good (frozen game when a run
  started in Terminal). Fixed: `dt` clamped at 0, Terminal guarded, and the
  frame loop now catches render errors so one bug cannot freeze the game.
- New worlds (18 total): Möbius Strip (3D, `MobiusTopology`: seam mirrors x;
  camera/controls track the side; left/right verified on screen before and
  after the seam), Deep Space (wrapping, drifting asteroids that never move
  into the snake or the 3 cells ahead of its head), Vertigo (board turns 90°
  every 6 s, arrows stay screen-relative), Checkmate (chess pieces as
  obstacles), Aquarium (kelp/coral obstacles, decorative fish and bubbles).
- Tests: 33 pass (placement, Möbius seam, world registry/order).
