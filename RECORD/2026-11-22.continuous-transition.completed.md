# Continuous world transition

Follow-up to `2026-11-22.snake-game.completed.md`.

## 2026-11-22

- Request: make the world change continuous instead of pausing on a banner.
- Implementation:
  - The 5th apple sets `pendingWarp`; after that frame is drawn, `warp()`
    snapshots the old world (WebGL canvas + 2D canvas) and swaps worlds
    without leaving the `playing` phase.
  - The snake keeps its length and is placed at the free cell closest to its
    old normalised position (`uvOf`), heading the same on-screen direction,
    with 4 free cells ahead (`placeSnake` in `src/main.js`). Falls back to the
    world's spawn.
  - Worlds' `render()` now returns the head's screen position; the old frame is
    drawn on top with a wobbly circular hole growing from the head (1.2 s), with
    a neon rim. A non-blocking toast names the new world and its controls.
  - Arriving through a portal skips the start delay (no terminal boot text).
- Verified: unit tests pass; headless Chromium tour through all 8 worlds and
  back to world 1 stays in `playing` with no console errors; transition
  screenshots reviewed.
